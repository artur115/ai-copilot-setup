# 06 · Domain and website

**By hand first:** in dash.cloudflare.com → **Domain Registration → Register Domains**, search your name, buy the `.dev` (about $12/year, sold at cost), turn on auto-renew.

```text
Build my personal site for <yourname>.dev in a new private GitHub repo called
"website".
- Content comes from my resume (I'll upload it) and LinkedIn headline.
- Design: I like <paste 1-2 links from kage.design or another gallery>. Use the
  layout idea, not their branding, with my own colors and fonts.
- Static HTML/CSS, fast, works on phones, with a /book page for my Calendly link.
- Deploy with Cloudflare Workers and connect the repo with Workers Builds, so
  every push to main goes live. Then point <yourname>.dev at it.
Show me a preview before the first deploy.
```
