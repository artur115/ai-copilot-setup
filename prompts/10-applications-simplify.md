# 10 · Applications (Simplify on Greenhouse forms)

**By hand:** add **Simplify Copilot** from the Chrome Web Store, build your profile once (upload the new resume, add your links). Autofill and the application tracker are free; Simplify+ is paid.

On each Greenhouse (or Lever, Workday) form: click **Autofill**, check every field, write the custom answers yourself, then submit.

```text
Here is a job description: <paste>. And my resume: <attach>.
1. Score my fit 1-10 and list the 3 strongest matches and the biggest gap.
2. Draft answers to these application questions: <paste>. Keep each under
   120 words, specific, and in my voice. No claims I can't back up.
3. Draft a 4-sentence note to the hiring manager with my booking link.
```
