# Design sites for web and app ideas

I keep these in a Chrome bookmarks folder, "App and website development". The method: pick one layout you like, give Claude the link, and ask for **your** content, colours and fonts in that layout. Use them as references, never copy another brand.

| Site | What it's for | How I used it |
|---|---|---|
| [kage.design](https://kage.design) | Landing-page designs with ready-made design briefs Claude can follow | My site started from a dark navy design, then a light lavender one |

## Prompt

```text
Here is a design I like: <link>. Study its layout, spacing, type and colour.
Build my <site / app screen> in that design language with my own content,
brand colours and fonts. Don't copy their logo, names or images.
Show me a preview before you deploy anything.
```
