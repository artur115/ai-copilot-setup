# Onboarding questions (ask once, in these rounds)

Ask one round at a time. Use multiple-choice buttons where offered (AskUserQuestion), free text otherwise.
Pre-fill from the resume and say "I took these from your resume; correct anything wrong". Never guess an answer the
user must give themselves (contact details, legal answers, demographics).

## Round 1: the jobs
1. Job titles to look for (suggest 5-8 from the resume, e.g. "Staff Data Engineer", "Data Engineering Manager").
2. Levels: individual contributor, manager, director (pick any).
3. Where: remote in the US, a city (which?), hybrid OK in which cities?
4. Lowest base pay to apply for, and the range to say when asked.
5. Skip: contract roles? roles needing a security clearance? anything else (industries, companies)?

## Round 2: work basics
1. Authorized to work in the US? Need visa sponsorship now or later?
2. Willing to relocate? Travel up to how much?
3. Earliest start / notice period.
4. Links: LinkedIn URL, website, GitHub, portfolio.

## Round 3: contact (typed by the user)
1. Email and phone to put on application forms. Explain: stored only in your private repo and used only in forms.
2. Optional: street address, city, county and zip (some job sites won't submit without them). "Skip" is fine: those jobs get logged for you.

## Round 4: experience numbers (for "how many years of X" questions)
1. Years in the field overall, years managing people, largest team.
2. Years with each main tool on the resume (suggest the list). Anything they've used that isn't on the resume yet?

## Round 5: standard form questions
1. Voluntary self-identification: gender, race/ethnicity, veteran status, disability. "Prefer not to say" is a fine answer for each.
2. Government official (last 5 years) or close relative of one? Conflict of interest with a senior leader at a company connected to the employer? Bound by a non-compete?
3. Consent to background checks? Drug screening? Accept arbitration agreements and privacy notices when required to apply?
4. Pronouns, if a form asks (optional).

## Round 6: how much Claude does alone
1. Submit applications without asking you? (yes / no: send me a daily shortlist instead)
2. LinkedIn: send connection requests and messages to recruiters? (send / draft only / no)
3. Create accounts on job sites when needed? (yes / no)
4. Runs per day and times (at least 2; suggest 8:17 AM and 4:17 PM local; 3 runs adds 12:17 PM).
5. Applications per run (suggest 15; LinkedIn Easy Apply is capped at 25 a day by the routine).
6. Tracker: create a new Google Sheet, or use an existing one (paste the link)?
